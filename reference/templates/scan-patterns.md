## Patterns

One row per pattern. `Status` is `Established`, `Emerging`, or `Fading` — that
column replaces separate sections for emerging and resolved themes; for
`Fading`, say what changed in `Recurrence`. `Where It Shows Up` lists the
programs, teams, or systems. A pattern on three or more surfaces is
cross-cutting — bold it; that is usually the highest-leverage intervention.

|---|---|---|---|---|

## What This Means

Three lines at most, and only where the implication is not already obvious
from the row. Name the pattern, then state what should change. Omit when the
table speaks for itself.

## Asks

Concrete and actionable — decision, escalation, or structural change, with a
named owner. A generic ask is worse than no ask.
