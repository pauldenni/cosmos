## Landscape

One row per active risk. `Trend` carries New / Increasing / Stable /
Decreasing / Resolved — that column is how new, worsening, and closing risks
get reported. They do not also get their own sections. `Note` is one clause.

| Risk | Severity | Trend | Owner | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## What Moved

Only risks whose trend changed since the last scan. One line each: what drove
the change, and what would arrest it. Name the risk; do not restate its row.

## Concentration

Where risk clusters — by owner, by program, by system. Name single-threaded
points, and any risk that keeps reopening instead of closing. Chronic risks
belong here, as structural signal, not as a second pass through the roster.

## Asks

What needs decision, escalation, or air cover. Named person, concrete request,
timing. Omit if the landscape needs nothing from anyone.
