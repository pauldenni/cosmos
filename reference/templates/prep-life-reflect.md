## How the Week Felt

Not what happened — how it felt. Energy, presence, balance between the parts of your life. One honest paragraph. Were you someone you'd want to be around?

## Where Time Went

Where your non-work hours actually went, not where you intended them to go. The `Gap` column carries the read. Do not add a sentence restating the table.

| Where you spent it | Where you wanted to spend it | Gap |
|---|---|---|

## Life Area Pulse

One row per area with real signal this week. Skip the rest — six rows every week is scaffolding, not a read. `Pulse` is one of: `thriving`, `stable`, `drifting`, `neglected`. `Note` is one line: who you showed up for, what your body is actually telling you, what went quiet.

| Area | Pulse | Note |
|---|---|---|

## What You Need to Face

The thing that keeps not happening, and the honest reason why — discomfort, energy, a competing priority, or something you haven't said out loud. One item, two at most.

If nothing qualifies this week, write one line saying so. An empty section is a valid result.

## What Mattered Most

Not an accomplishment. A moment, a conversation, an experience — the thing you'd want to remember from this week.

## Carry Forward

What has genuinely improved over the last several weeks. If a pattern from "What You Need to Face" keeps returning, note the recurrence in one clause rather than restating it.

Close with one orienting line for the week ahead — a posture, not a to-do list.
