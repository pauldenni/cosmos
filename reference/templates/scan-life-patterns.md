## Patterns

One line per pattern, tagged `Holding`, `Persisting`, `Avoiding`, or `Faded`.

- `Holding` — genuinely improving or consistently strong. Say what changed.
- `Persisting` — recurs despite awareness. Cite the first reflect that named
  it and the most recent one that named it the same way.
- `Avoiding` — slips out of reflections, or gets renamed rather than solved.
- `Faded` — stopped recurring. Say what changed.

## The Gap

actually spends time and energy. The most important section. One short
paragraph, no softening.
