# Career Ladder

**This is a placeholder. Replace it with your organization's actual ladder before using `/prep calibration`.**

`/prep calibration <person>` rates someone against this document. Every rating must cite evidence from it, so a generic ladder produces a generic — and therefore useless — calibration. If your org has a published ladder, paste it here verbatim. If it doesn't, write down the expectations you actually hold people to; that is more honest than borrowing someone else's rubric.

Until this file has real content, `/prep calibration` will correctly report **Insufficient Evidence** across the board.

## What this file needs

A level-by-level definition of expectations. At minimum, for each level:

- **Scope** — what surface area the person is accountable for
- **Autonomy** — how much direction they need, and from whom
- **Influence** — who they move, and how far outside their own team
- **Craft / technical depth** — what "good" looks like in the work itself
- **People impact** — how they raise the people around them

## Companion file

`reference/org/pm-calibration-template.md` is the output shape `/prep calibration` fills in. That one is generic and ready to use — it does not need editing.

## A note on using it

The template's own pattern check is worth honoring: three or more axes rated *Above* means investigate promotion readiness; three or more *Below* means find the root cause; **two or more Insufficient Evidence means the calibration is not finished** — say so, and name what you need to close the gap.
