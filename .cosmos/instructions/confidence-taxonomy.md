# Confidence Taxonomy

Confidence measures:
how strongly a pattern is supported longitudinally.

# High Confidence

Requirements:
- repeated across multiple weeks
- appears across multiple sources
- repeated executive visibility
- repeated escalation presence
- recurring operational impact

# Medium Confidence

Requirements:
- recurring but not fully stabilized
- visible across limited sources
- trend emerging but not fully persistent

# Low Confidence

Requirements:
- early signal only
- limited recurrence
- unclear persistence
- insufficient longitudinal evidence
