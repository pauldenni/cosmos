# Recurring Signal Classification Rules

## Recurring Blocker

Definition:
A repeatedly unresolved impediment affecting execution.

## Dependency Friction

Definition:
Repeated coordination difficulty between systems or teams.

## Organizational Friction

Definition:
Repeated operational inefficiency caused by unclear ownership or coordination structure.

## Delivery Confidence Shift

Definition:
Meaningful change in perceived execution predictability.

## Leadership Narrative Drift

Definition:
Meaningful change in executive framing over time.

## Emerging Risk

Definition:
A signal increasing in frequency or operational significance.

## Stabilizing Area

Definition:
An operational area showing improving consistency or reduced escalation.
